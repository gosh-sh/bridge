## What the pack answers

1. **VKs**: all three AN→ETH circuit VKs in canonical `SerdeFormat::RawBytesUnchecked` form, paired with the `BaseCircuitParams` needed to decode them.
2. **Sample proofs**: one synthetic per circuit, generated by `bridge-snark-utils/src/bin/export_*.rs` against `bridge_test_data_gen::generate_bridge_test_data(bk_set_size=10)`.
3. **Public-input layout**: documented per circuit in `01_an_to_eth_verification_keys/README.md` with concrete decimal values.
4. **Integration shape**: how we drive her keygen, prove, verify — `keys.rs`, `layer_hashes_keys.rs`, `prover.rs`, `layer_hashes_prover.rs`, `verifier.rs`.
5. **Cross-circuit consistency**: `bound_test_data.rs` + `04_bound_scenario/` show that 1A and 2 emit matching `(block_id, bk_set_poseidon)` pairs for the same scenario.
6. **TVM-side wire format**: `halo2_tvm_bundle.rs` is our producer-side encoding for the proposed `ZKHALO2VERIFYWITHVK` opcode (consumer side in `tvm-sdk` branch `serhii/verhalo2shplonk-skeleton`).
7. **Our independent ETH→AN circuit**: full `deposit-prover/` source for her opinion on the future of that path.

## Reproducibility

Pack was assembled deterministically from:

- `crates/bridge-snark-utils/params/{primary,fallback,layer_hashes}_{vk,config_params}.bin/json`
- `crates/bridge-snark-utils/proofs/{primary,fallback,layer-hashes}/{proof,instances}.bin`
- `crates/bridge-snark-utils/proofs/{primary,fallback,layer-hashes}/halo2_proof.json`
- `crates/bridge-snark-utils/proofs/bound/{bound_scenario.json, primary/groth16_output.json, layer-hashes/groth16_output.json}`
- `crates/bridge-snark-utils/src/*.rs` + `Cargo.toml` (→ `orchestrator_Cargo.toml`)
- `deposit-prover/src/*.rs` + `Cargo.toml` + `README.md` (→ `original_README.md`)
- `docs/zk_halo2_an_side_design.md`

The sample proofs are **non-deterministic** because `bridge_test_data_gen` uses `rand::thread_rng()` — rebuilding the pack with `cargo run --release --bin export_{primary,fallback,layer_hashes,bound_block}_proof` will produce structurally identical but bitwise-different `proof.bin` / `instances.bin` / `halo2_proof.json`. VK + config_params and the source-code files are deterministic.
